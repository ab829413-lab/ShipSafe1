DDP shipping setup checklist

☐ Confirm whether your ship-from country requires DDP for
   US orders (non-US sellers shipping physical goods do).

☐ Open Shop Manager and check for Etsy's US tariffs
   calculator in your listing form.

☐ Look up accurate HS codes for your product categories
   at hts.usitc.gov.

☐ Add clear, specific customs descriptions to your shipping
   profiles — avoid vague terms like "gift" or "sample."

☐ Check whether your shipping carrier offers a DDP option,
   or use one of Etsy's partner shipping services.

☐ Recalculate your US prices to include the estimated
   duty cost (see the worksheet above).

☐ If you ship to the EU too, check its July 2026 customs
   changes separately — the rules aren't the same.

☐ Re-check this list if you add new product categories or
   start shipping from a new country.
